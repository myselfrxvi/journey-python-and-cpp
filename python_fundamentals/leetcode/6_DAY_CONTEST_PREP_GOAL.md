# 6-Day Contest 519 Prep Goal (Sep 7 - Sep 12)

**Contest Target:** LeetCode Weekly Contest 519  
**Contest Time:** Sunday, Sep 13, 08:00 AM (GMT +05:30)  
**Primary Objective:** Solve at least 2 out of 4 problems (Q1 + Q2) by mastering fast logic identification and Python implementation.

---

## The 6-Day Curriculum

### Day 1 (Monday, Sep 7): Hash Maps & Two Pointers (The Q1/Q2 Guarantee)
- **Goal:** Solve Q1 in under 15 minutes.
- **Key Logic Skills:**
  - Frequency counting with Python's `collections.Counter` and `defaultdict`.
  - In-place two pointers (left & right, fast & slow).
  - Identifying when sorting the array unlocks O(N) or O(N log N) logic.
- **Daily Drill:** 1 Easy + 1 Medium unseen problem with strict 3-minute pattern callout.

---

### Day 2 (Tuesday, Sep 8): Sliding Window & Prefix Sums
- **Goal:** Master every question containing "contiguous subarray" or "substring".
- **Key Logic Skills:**
  - Dynamic Sliding Window (expand right, contract left when condition breaks).
  - Fixed-size Sliding Window.
  - Prefix Sum hash map technique (e.g. Subarray Sum Equals K).
- **Daily Drill:** 2 Medium problems focusing on window condition mechanics.

---

### Day 3 (Wednesday, Sep 9): Heaps & Monotonic Stacks
- **Goal:** Master "Top K elements" and "Next Greater Element" problems.
- **Key Logic Skills:**
  - Python `heapq` patterns: min-heap vs max-heap (negative numbers trick).
  - Monotonic increasing vs decreasing stack.
- **Daily Drill:** 2 Medium problems.

---

### Day 4 (Thursday, Sep 10): Binary Search on Answer
- **Goal:** Solve the most common sneaky Q2/Q3 pattern.
- **Key Logic Skills:**
  - Recognizing when the answer has a monotonic condition: `check(mid) -> True/False`.
  - Writing the binary search template without infinite loop bugs (`low <= high` vs `low < high`).
- **Daily Drill:** 2 Medium binary search on answer problems.

---

### Day 5 (Friday, Sep 11): Backtracking & Dynamic Programming Decisions
- **Goal:** Conquer decision-tree problems without fear.
- **Key Logic Skills:**
  - The 3 backtracking steps: Choose -> Explore -> Unchoose.
  - Memoization with Python's `@lru_cache` and manual hash tables.
- **Daily Drill:** 1 Backtracking + 1 DP problem.

---

### Day 6 (Saturday, Sep 12): Full Mock Contest (90 Minutes Timed)
- **Goal:** Simulate the exact test conditions before Sunday morning.
- **Exam Protocol:**
  - 4 unseen questions on a 90-minute timer.
  - Strategy: Lock in Q1 (first 20 mins) -> Target Q2 (next 40 mins) -> Bonus push on Q3.
  - Review edge cases, 0-indexing, empty arrays, and off-by-one errors.

---

## The Daily 3-Step Training Rule

For every problem we do this week:
1. **No coding for the first 3 minutes.**
2. **Name the pattern and time complexity out loud based on constraints.**
3. **Write 3 bullet points of plain English logic, then code.**
